# KoshBank UPI Guide

> Fictional policy for the KoshGuard demo.

## Setting up UPI
Link your KoshBank account to any UPI app using your registered mobile number. You will set a 4 or 6 digit UPI PIN using your debit card details.

## Transaction limits
The UPI limit is 1,00,000 rupees per transaction and 1,00,000 rupees per day. Newly registered devices are limited to 5,000 rupees in the first 24 hours.

## Failed transactions
If money is debited but the UPI transaction fails, it is auto-reversed within 5 working days. If it is not reversed, raise a complaint in the UPI app or contact KoshBank customer care.

## Safety tips
Never share your UPI PIN. You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Collect requests from unknown people are a common fraud pattern: decline them.
