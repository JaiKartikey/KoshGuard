# KoshBank Debit Card: Blocking, Replacement and Limits

> Fictional policy for the KoshGuard demo.

## Blocking a lost or stolen card
If your debit card is lost or stolen, block it immediately through the KoshBank app (Cards > Block card), through net banking, or by calling the 24x7 helpline at 1800-000-0000.
Blocking is permanent and cannot be reversed. A temporary freeze option is available in the app if you only misplaced the card.
KoshBank staff will never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN or OTP.

## Replacement card
A replacement card can be requested from the app after blocking. The replacement fee is 200 rupees plus GST and the card is delivered to your registered address within 7 working days.

## Daily limits
The default daily ATM withdrawal limit is 50,000 rupees and the default daily purchase limit is 1,00,000 rupees. You can lower or raise these limits within the card's maximum from the app.

## Unauthorised transactions
Report any unauthorised transaction within 3 working days to get zero liability, as per the bank's customer protection policy. Reports made between 4 and 7 working days may carry limited liability.
