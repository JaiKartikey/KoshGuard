# KoshBank Fees and Charges Schedule

> Fictional charges for the KoshGuard demo. All charges are exclusive of GST.

## Savings account charges
Non-maintenance of average monthly balance: up to 300 rupees per month for Kosh Classic and up to 500 rupees per month for Kosh Premium.
Cheque book: the first 25 cheque leaves per year are free; thereafter 4 rupees per leaf.

## ATM charges
Five free transactions per month at KoshBank ATMs. Three free transactions per month at other banks' ATMs in metro cities and five in non-metro cities. After the free limit, 21 rupees per financial transaction.

## Card charges
Debit card annual fee: 150 rupees for Classic cards and 500 rupees for Premium cards. Replacement card fee: 200 rupees.

## Fund transfer charges
NEFT, RTGS and IMPS transfers made through the app or net banking are free. Branch-initiated NEFT costs 2.50 rupees for amounts up to 10,000 rupees.
