# KoshBank Savings Account Policy

> KoshBank is a fictional bank created for the KoshGuard demo. None of these terms describe a real bank.

## Account variants
KoshBank offers three savings accounts: Kosh Basic (zero balance), Kosh Classic and Kosh Premium.
Kosh Basic is a Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account with no minimum balance requirement and up to four free withdrawals a month.

## Minimum balance
Kosh Classic requires an average monthly balance (AMB) of 10,000 rupees in metro and urban branches and 5,000 rupees in rural branches.
Kosh Premium requires an average monthly balance of 25,000 rupees.
If the AMB is not maintained, a non-maintenance charge applies as listed in the Fees and Charges schedule.

## Interest rate
Savings interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly.
Balances up to 1 lakh rupees earn 3.0 percent per annum. Balances above 1 lakh rupees earn 3.5 percent per annum.

## Opening an account
You can open a savings account online through the KoshBank app with Aadhaar-based e-KYC and your PAN, or at any branch.
Accounts opened with OTP-based e-KYC are limited to a balance of 1 lakh rupees until full KYC is completed.

## Closing an account
To close an account, submit the account closure form at your home branch. Accounts closed within 14 days of opening are free; closure between 14 days and 12 months costs 500 rupees; closure after 12 months is free.
