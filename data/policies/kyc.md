# KoshBank KYC Requirements

> Fictional policy for the KoshGuard demo.

## Documents accepted
KYC needs proof of identity and proof of address. Accepted documents are Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, driving licence and NREGA job card. PAN or Form 60 is mandatory for opening an account.

## Video KYC
Full KYC can be completed online through video KYC with a KoshBank official between 9 am and 9 pm on all days. Keep your original PAN card ready.

## Periodic KYC update
KYC must be updated every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers. If there is no change in your details, you can submit a self-declaration through the app or by email.

## Incomplete KYC
If KYC is not updated by the due date, the account may be partially frozen: credits are allowed but debits are restricted until KYC is completed.
