# KoshBank Fixed Deposit Rates and Rules

> Fictional rates for the KoshGuard demo. Not an offer from any real bank.

## Interest rates for general customers
The current fixed deposit rates for deposits below 3 crore rupees are:
7 days to 45 days: 3.50 percent per annum.
46 days to 179 days: 5.25 percent per annum.
180 days to 1 year: 6.00 percent per annum.
Above 1 year up to 3 years: 7.10 percent per annum.
Above 3 years up to 10 years: 6.75 percent per annum.

## Senior citizen rates
Customers aged 60 years or more receive an additional 0.50 percent per annum on all tenures.

## Minimum deposit and tenure
The minimum fixed deposit amount is 5,000 rupees. Tenure ranges from 7 days to 10 years.

## Premature withdrawal
Premature withdrawal is allowed. A penalty of 1 percent is deducted from the rate applicable for the period the deposit actually stayed with the bank. Tax-saver fixed deposits cannot be withdrawn before 5 years.

## Tax deducted at source
TDS is deducted when interest income exceeds the limit set under the Income Tax Act. Submit Form 15G or 15H at the start of the financial year if you are eligible to avoid TDS.
