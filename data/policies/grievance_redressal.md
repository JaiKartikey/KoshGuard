# KoshBank Grievance Redressal Policy

> Fictional policy for the KoshGuard demo.

## How to raise a complaint
Raise a complaint through the app (Help > Raise a request), by email to care@koshbank.example, by calling 1800-000-0000, or at any branch. You will receive a complaint reference number.

## Escalation levels
Level 1: Customer care resolves most complaints within 7 working days.
Level 2: If unresolved, escalate to the Nodal Officer at nodal.officer@koshbank.example. The Nodal Officer responds within 10 working days.
Level 3: If you are still not satisfied, or receive no reply within 30 days, you can approach the RBI Ombudsman through the RBI Complaint Management System.

## Turnaround times
Failed ATM transactions where cash was not dispensed are reversed within 5 working days. Compensation of 100 rupees per day is paid for delays beyond this period.
