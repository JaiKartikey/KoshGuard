# KoshBank Loans FAQ

> Fictional terms for the KoshGuard demo.

## Personal loans
Personal loans are available from 50,000 rupees to 25 lakh rupees for tenures of 12 to 60 months. Interest rates start at 10.75 percent per annum and depend on your credit profile. The processing fee is up to 2 percent of the loan amount.

## Home loans
Home loan rates start at 8.50 percent per annum, linked to the repo rate. Tenure can be up to 30 years. There is no prepayment penalty on floating-rate home loans for individual borrowers.

## Eligibility
Salaried applicants must be between 21 and 60 years old with a minimum net monthly income of 25,000 rupees. A credit score of 700 or above improves your chances of approval.

## EMI and repayment
EMIs are auto-debited from your KoshBank account on the 5th of every month. A bounce charge of 500 rupees applies to each failed EMI debit.
